IQCS auditor registration

KCLI delivers training and assesses learners. To register your personal qualification, submit a separate application to the International Qualification Certification Service (IQCS). IQCS assesses your evidence against the standard and grade you select.

Prepare your application International applicants Verify KCLI’s designation

Applying from Malaysia or elsewhere in Southeast Asia

IQCS publishes the registration procedure in English and an English fee schedule. Its English and Korean pages list different audit experience thresholds and fees. Before applying, ask IQCS which rules, fees and billing currency apply to your country, standard and grade.

Verify the training provider

KCLI’s designation number is KRT0184. Look it up in the IQCS provider directory (record in Korean).

Prepare your application

  1. Select a standard and grade. Choose the management system relevant to your work and check the standards available for IQCS registration.
  2. Complete the training and assessment. Retain the training provider’s certificate showing that you passed.
  3. Gather the supporting evidence. Depending on your route and grade, this can include education and employment records, audit logs or a witnessed audit report.
  4. Apply directly to IQCS. IQCS reviews the application and supporting documents. Follow its instructions to complete registration after approval.

What does passing a course establish?

The KCLI training certificate is one item of evidence for your personal application. Submit it with the other evidence required for your selected grade through the IQCS application portal (sign-in required).

Choose a registration grade

Associate auditor
For first registration, prepare the application form and the certificate showing that you passed auditor training.
Auditor
Check whether the audit-log route (QB) or the witnessed-audit route (VB) applies and document the required experience.
Lead auditor
Document your audit experience, including your role as an audit team leader.

What happens after you apply?

  1. IQCS receives your application.
  2. You submit your supporting documents.
  3. IQCS reviews the documents.
  4. You provide any further information it requests.
  5. IQCS verifies the information with the client.
  6. IQCS approves registration when the requirements are met.
  7. IQCS invoices the annual fee.
  8. IQCS sends the certificate and qualification card.

Documents by application type

First registration as a associate auditor
Application form and auditor training pass certificate.
First registration as an auditor or lead auditor
Application form, training pass certificate, résumé or job description, and proof of graduation from your highest level of education. Add audit logs for the grade under the QB route or a witnessed audit report under the VB route.
Upgrade to a higher grade
Application form, a copy of your qualification card or registration certificate, and audit logs for the grade you are seeking.
Transfer your registration
Application form and a copy of your qualification card or registration certificate.

Experience, audit evidence and renewal

The following thresholds summarise the Korean-language registration guidance linked below. International applicants should confirm the applicable requirements with IQCS.

Education and work experience
IQCS lists completion of secondary education or above and four years of work experience, including at least two years related to the standard. Check which requirements apply to your chosen grade.
QB route
For auditor grade, document at least three audits totalling seven or more days in the preceding three years. For lead auditor, document at least four audits totalling 11 or more days in that period, including at least four days as team leader. Both grades require participation in at least one initial or recertification audit.
VB route
Submit the VB application, audit schedule, training pass certificate, job description and proof of graduation. IQCS arranges a witnessed audit at a selected organisation. Successful assessment leads to auditor registration.
Auditor to lead auditor
In the preceding three years, lead at least two audits totalling four or more days. Include at least one initial or recertification audit.
Lead auditor to verification auditor
As a lead auditor, conduct at least two audits totalling five or more days over one year. Lead at least one initial or recertification audit.
Renewal and continuing professional development (CPD)
Registered grades other than associate auditor renew every three years. The procedure lists 30 hours of CPD over that period, including at least 15 hours relating to the management system. The IQCS renewal notice of 14 February 2025 (Korean) asks applicants to submit a log of 30 hours of CPD for the standard under renewal.

IQCS accepts first-, second- and third-party audit experience. Verify your route against the official IQCS registration procedure (Korean).

Registration fees

IQCS publishes these personal registration fees on its Korean-language schedule, in Korean won (KRW). The English schedule lists different amounts. Confirm the applicable charges with IQCS before payment. For KCLI course tuition, contact the training coordinator.

A dash means the schedule does not specify an amount. Additional fees include a grade upgrade (KRW 80,000), a witnessed audit (KRW 350,000 plus travel) and a qualification card (KRW 10,000 annually).

When a associate auditor registers as an auditor during the period covered by the associate auditor fee, IQCS credits that fee in full. A transfer from another registration body carries a 30% reduction in the first year’s annual fee. Confirm the applicable amount in the official IQCS fee schedule (Korean) before applying.
