About the institute

Founded in 2020 in Seoul, KCLI trains management system auditors and publishes guides for working professionals. Our courses bring together standards interpretation, audit planning, case workshops and assessment.

Our approach

In a workshop, learners review a record, decide what it shows and document how it supports their finding. The institute explains the assessment criteria before learners begin.

The KCLI symbol

Olive, blue and sky blue represent land, sea and sky. The three shapes share a baseline and lean 15° to the right, expressing the resolve to put judgement into practice.

Setting goals and managing quality in the AI era

Choose a result you want to improve at work. Decide which records would show progress, then use those records to check the measures and explanations an AI tool suggests.

Exercise · Check an AI attendance recommendation

In this fictional class, the training and reporting deadlines have passed. Only learner A is marked as having completed the course. An AI tool recommends a make-up session for B and C.

  • A attended and is listed in the completion register.
  • B attended but has no entry in the register.
  • C was absent and has no entry in the register.

Who needs a make-up session? How would you measure course completion and the quality of the register separately?

See a worked answer

Offer C a make-up session. Check B against the attendance sheet and correct the register. Two of the three learners completed the course. Of those two, only one had a completion record entered on time.

For the next class, aim to record every confirmed completion by the reporting deadline. Assign someone to compare the two records at that deadline and report whether the goal was met.

Public officials

Review a fictional public enquiries register. Identify on-time replies and cases reopened because the answer was incomplete. Choose measures for both timeliness and response quality, then use one case to explain your assessment.
