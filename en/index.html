ISO auditor training in Seoul

Korea Cyber Literacy Institute (KCLI, 한국사이버리터러시연구원) is an ISO auditor training provider in Gasan, Geumcheon-gu, Seoul. Training covers interpreting management system requirements, practising audits with case studies, and assessment.

Which ISO auditor course fits my work?

Choose ISO 9001 if you work with quality processes and customer requirements. Choose ISO/IEC 42001 if you work with AI risk, impact assessment or governance.

How do I register as an IQCS auditor?

After passing the KCLI course, apply separately to IQCS. IQCS reviews the supporting documents against the requirements for your selected standard and auditor grade. Consult the official IQCS registration procedure (Korean) before applying.

The IQCS provider directory (Korean) lists KCLI under designation number KRT0184, issued on 16 November 2023. KCLI's designation summary (Korean) lists seven ISO training and examination areas and ESG Auditor. Check current scope and renewal status with IQCS. ESG Auditor and ISO/UNDP 53001 are separate entries.

Where can I check dates, tuition and enrolment?

For ISO 9001 or ISO/IEC 42001, contact KCLI to confirm session dates, the application deadline, availability, delivery language, training location or format, tuition and the entry requirements. IQCS lists auditor registration charges in its separate fee schedule (Korean).
