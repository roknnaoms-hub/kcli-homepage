About the institute

Founded in 2020 in Seoul, KCLI trains management system auditors and publishes guides for working professionals. Our courses bring together standards interpretation, audit planning, case workshops and assessment.

Our approach

In a workshop, learners review a record, decide what it shows and document how it supports their finding. The institute explains the assessment criteria before learners begin.

The KCLI symbol

Olive, blue and sky blue represent land, sea and sky. The three shapes share a baseline and lean 15° to the right, expressing the resolve to put judgement into practice.

AI management system auditor training

The three-day ISO/IEC 42001 course covers the standard and related documents, certification audit principles, AI concepts and evidence. Learners complete a written examination and a case workshop assessment.

After training

Once you pass your course, you can apply separately to IQCS for auditor registration. IQCS reviews your evidence against the requirements for the standard and grade you select.

ISO training for Southeast Asia

KCLI is planning ISO auditor training for Malaysia and other Southeast Asian countries. Individuals, employers and local training organisations can enquire about a course or discuss hosting a group.

Plan a session with KCLI

Share the country and city, the ISO standard, the number of learners, the teaching language you need and your preferred dates. KCLI will discuss delivery format, assessments, fees and arrangements before confirming a session.

Contact KCLI

For an individual course, tell us which standard interests you, your preferred dates and the language you need. We can confirm the teaching language, fees and entry requirements for that session.

For institutional training or a partnership, include your organisation, group size and learning goals.
