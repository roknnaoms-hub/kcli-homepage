A welcome from the director

Welcome to KCLI. Our aim is to train auditors who interpret management system requirements accurately and can explain what evidence supports each finding.

We work through requirements, audit procedures, case exercises and assessment. In workshops you practise comparing evidence with the audit criteria and recording the basis for your judgement. We explain the assessment criteria in advance and apply them consistently. After the course we help you understand the requirements for IQCS auditor registration.

Myeongseop Oh, Director

Institute profile

KCLI was established on 15 January 2020. Based in Seoul’s Gasan Digital Industrial Complex, it provides ISO auditor training, AI and cyber literacy education, and knowledge services. Learners interpret standards and technology in context, evaluate information sources and apply what they learn to a practical case.

The KCLI symbol

KCLI draws on the Korean tradition of heaven, earth and humanity. Olive represents land and the grounding of judgement in facts. Blue represents the sea, exchange and cooperation between people. Sky blue represents the wider perspective needed to see change.

The shared baseline and 15° forward tilt express initiative and a common direction. Each colour retains its identity, reflecting the Confucian idea of harmony with difference.

How auditor training works

  1. Interpret the purpose of the clauses and decide what an auditor should check.
  2. Plan the audit, prepare questions and collect evidence.
  3. Use a case to match evidence to the criteria and record a finding.
  4. Complete the course assessments and review the separate IQCS registration process.

Quality and impartiality policies

Quality

We publish entry requirements, assessment criteria and procedures in advance. We review the applicable standards and develop instructor competence. Feedback from learners and other interested parties informs improvements to teaching, assessment and guidance.

Impartiality

We apply the same criteria to participants under the same conditions. We manage conflicts of interest and protect assessments from personal, financial and commercial pressures. We review objections against the evidence and applicable criteria.

Who does what in ISO certification?

ISO
Develops and publishes international standards. ISO’s guide to certification
KSA and KATS
The Korean Standards Association provides standards information and education. The Korean Agency for Technology and Standards represents Korea at ISO. ISO member listing · KSA services (Korean)
KAB and SCC
Accreditation bodies recognise competence for specified activities. KAB publishes GERMAN CERT’s management system scope. SCC publishes IQCS’s personnel certification scope. KAB directory (Korean) · SCC scope (PDF)
IQCS
Reviews individual applicants’ qualifications and manages auditor registration. Read the registration guide
GERMAN CERT
Audits organisations, reviews audit results independently and issues a management system certificate when it approves certification. Certification services (Korean)
KCLI
Provides auditor education, workshops and assessment as an IQCS-designated training institute.

From training to an organisation’s certificate

  1. Complete KCLI training and pass the assessment for the chosen standard.
  2. Submit course evidence and the required experience records to IQCS for the relevant auditor grade.
  3. Registered auditors who meet the competence requirements may be assigned to a certification body’s audit team.
  4. The certification body reviews the audit findings and decides whether to issue an organisational certificate.

International Qualification Certification Service

IQCS designates and monitors training providers, registers qualified auditors and operates personnel certification programmes. Its lead auditor course brochure covers 25 standards. The KCLI catalogue identifies the eight standards offered by this institute.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of institute is KCLI?

KCLI is an IQCS-designated auditor training provider in Seoul. It teaches requirements interpretation, audit procedure and case-based evidence assessment. Check the designation.

Which standards can I study?

Quality, environment, occupational health and safety, business continuity, information security, privacy, AI and the Sustainable Development Goals. Browse the eight standards.

How do I register as an auditor?

After passing training, apply to IQCS with the documents for your intended standard and grade. See grades, evidence and fees.

Where can I find dates, fees and application deadlines?

Contact KCLI to confirm the session, tuition, format and language. IQCS sets the separate auditor registration fees. Prepare an enquiry.

How long is the ISO/IEC 42001 course?

The current course is three days. It includes case workshops and a written examination.

Can an organisation request a group course in Southeast Asia?

Yes. KCLI is planning regional delivery. Send the proposed country and city, standard, group size, language and dates so the institute can discuss arrangements. Discuss a group.

Does sending an enquiry enrol me?

The website prepares an email draft. KCLI confirms registration, availability and payment arrangements directly with you.
