ISO auditor training in Seoul

Korea Cyber Literacy Institute (KCLI, 한국사이버리터러시연구원) is an ISO auditor training provider in Seoul's Gasan Digital Industrial Complex. Training covers interpreting management system requirements, practising audits with case studies, and assessment.

Which ISO auditor course fits my work?

Choose ISO 9001 if you work with quality processes and customer requirements. Choose ISO/IEC 42001 if you work with AI risk, impact assessment or governance.

Which standards does KCLI cover?

  • ISO 9001:2026

    Quality management systems

    Learn to trace a customer requirement through a work process and use performance records to assess improvement.

    ISO publication

  • ISO 14001:2026

    Environmental management systems

    Identify environmental aspects and review the records an organisation uses to track compliance obligations and environmental performance.

    ISO publication

  • ISO 45001:2018 · Amd 1:2024

    Occupational health and safety management systems

    Review how a workplace identifies hazards, involves workers and checks whether risk controls are effective.

    ISO publication

  • ISO 22301:2019 · Amd 1:2024

    Business continuity management systems

    Connect a business impact analysis to continuity plans and review how an organisation tests its response to disruption.

    ISO publication

  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022 · Amd 1:2024

    Information security management systems

    Follow an information security risk from assessment to control selection and examine evidence that the control operates.

    ISO publication

  • ISO/IEC 27701:2025

    Privacy information management systems

    Identify who is responsible for processing personal information and review the controls used to manage privacy risks.

    ISO publication

  • ISO/IEC 42001:2023

    Artificial intelligence management systems

    Examine AI risks and impacts, identify who is accountable and review the evidence behind decisions throughout the AI system lifecycle.

    ISO publication

  • ISO/UNDP 53001:2026

    Management systems for the Sustainable Development Goals

    Examine how an organisation selects relevant goals, incorporates them into its activities and measures progress.

    ISO publication

How do I register as an IQCS auditor?

After passing the KCLI course, apply separately to IQCS. IQCS reviews the supporting documents against the requirements for your selected standard and auditor grade. Consult the official IQCS registration procedure (Korean) before applying.

Check KCLI's designation number, KRT0184, in the IQCS provider directory (Korean).

Where can I check dates, tuition and enrolment?

For any of the eight standards, contact KCLI to confirm session dates, the application deadline, availability, delivery language, training location or format, tuition and the entry requirements. IQCS lists auditor registration charges in its separate fee schedule (Korean).
