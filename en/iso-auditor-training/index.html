ISO auditor training in Seoul

Korea Cyber Literacy Institute (KCLI, 한국사이버리터러시연구원) is an ISO auditor training provider in Seoul's Gasan Digital Industrial Complex. Training covers interpreting management system requirements, practising audits with case studies, and assessment.

Which ISO auditor course fits my work?

Choose ISO 9001 if you work with quality processes and customer requirements. Choose ISO/IEC 42001 if you work with AI risk, impact assessment or governance.

Before enrolling

Check that the standard applies to the work you want to audit. Ask KCLI which edition it teaches, the contact hours, assessment format and conditions for issuing a training certificate. If you intend to register as an auditor, check the requirements for your IQCS grade.

How do I register as an IQCS auditor?

After passing the KCLI course, apply separately to IQCS. IQCS reviews the supporting documents against the requirements for your selected standard and auditor grade. Consult the official IQCS registration procedure before applying. Applicants from Malaysia or elsewhere in Southeast Asia can consult our international registration guidance.

Check KCLI's designation number, KRT0184, in the IQCS provider directory (record in Korean).
