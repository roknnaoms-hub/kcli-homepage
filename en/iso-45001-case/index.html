What evidence can you trace using one incident ID?

An ISO 45001 audit examines the organisation’s occupational health and safety management system. In this exercise, use the safety information system to link one incident report to the risk assessment, action records and site observations.

  1. Incident report and worker input

    Clauses 5.4 and 10.2

    Ask the driver and the pedestrian separately where they were and what each could see. Compare the locations they identify with the reported time to establish which activities overlapped.

  2. Risk assessment and choice of controls

    Clauses 6.1.2 and 8.1.2

    Check whether the risk assessment and route plan account for a potential collision at shift change. Find out whether rerouting or physical separation was considered and how workers’ views influenced the choice of control.

  3. Implementation and records

    Clauses 7.5 and 10.2

    Identify the person responsible and the due date. If the action was to change a route, use the incident ID to trace the revised layout, installation records and communication to each shift.

  4. Effectiveness and closure

    Clauses 9.1.1 and 10.2

    Observe the routes vehicles and workers take at day and night shift changes. Record your observations and worker interviews. If risk remains, revisit the risk assessment and action plan.

What would you record as the auditor?

The day shift attendance register is the only attachment. Write one sentence stating what the evidence establishes and one identifying what you still need to check.

Show a sample audit note

Verified facts The register confirms attendance at day shift safety training. It provides insufficient evidence to determine whether the collision risk at shift change has decreased.

Further checks Compare the actual routes on both shifts and the driver and pedestrian interviews with the risk assessment. Use the findings to assess effectiveness and decide whether the evidence supports closure.
